# Document review

Review the document you were given against the task and the standards.
Say whether it is ready, and for each problem give the place, what is wrong and what would fix it.
Separate what must change from what you merely suggest.
Do not rewrite the document yourself.
Write the required `outcome` port to `outcome.json` in your working directory.
Use the strict `guildhall.review-outcome/1` JSON format, with your actual verdict, findings and dispositions and the exact bound document revisions.
The verdict is `approved`, `changes_requested` or `rejected`, not `APPROVE`.
