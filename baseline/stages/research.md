# Research

Learn what the change will touch before anyone plans it.
Read the parts of the repository the task concerns, its documents and its tests.
Record how the relevant code works today, what the change must keep working and any risk you found.
Cite files and lines rather than paraphrasing them.
Write the required `notes` port as a UTF-8 Markdown document at `notes.md` in your working directory.
List it in result `outputs` as `{"path":"notes.md"}`; see the generated Required outputs section for a minimal example.
