# `internal/interp`

## Contract

`Engine.Advance` checks `config.CheckArtisanAdapters` before event intake or any mutation: an Artisan step that resolves no profile is refused with the remedy `config.CheckArtisanAdapters` gives.
An Artisan step launches, renders and is narrowed under its effective profile (`workflow.EffectiveProfile`): the profile it names, else its role profile, so the synthetic `<step>-rebase` runs under `rebaser` and `<step>-rebase-review` under the review step's profile or `reviewer`.
A missing profile is `config.ErrMissingAdapter`, not an implicit fake; the refusal names the step and directs the user to declare its role profile or set a profile on that step.
Explicit fake/mock profiles remain usable, including tests; there is no profile-less exemption for test binaries.
`TestAdvanceRunsAProfilelessStepUnderItsRoleProfile` launches a plan step that names no profile under `planner`, and `TestAdvanceRefusesProfilelessLegacyTask` refuses it in a config without `planner`, which only a config built without `config.Parse` can be, over both records backends, recording and launching no attempts.
A minor conflict escalates for want of a rebaser only in such a config (`TestValidateRebaseWithoutRebaser`, the "no rebaser profile" case of `TestQueueTier2Escalates`); `TestShippedRebaserAndRoleReviewerResolveMinorConflicts` runs the rebaser of a parsed config that declares none, and the narrowed review of a review step that names no profile under `reviewer`.

[Code-head output intake](../interpreter.md#code-head-output-intake) owns the derived Git output, workspace requirements, explicit-file compatibility and immutable replay contract.
`head_replay_test.go` exercises real Git over both records backends: running and launcher-finished successes, empty output lists, missing intake markers, untracked notes, staged/unstaged dirt, progression to review, and immutable replay after HEAD moves.
The feedback command `go test ./internal/interp -run '^TestBuildHeadReplay$' -count=1` failed before the implementation ([red](../evidence/build-head-output-2026-10-25-red.txt)) and passed afterwards ([green](../evidence/build-head-output-2026-10-25-green.txt)).
`head_validation_test.go` adds green-first controls for unchanged explicit-head validation and missing document-output errors.

`Engine.Advance`, the deterministic interpreter ([interpreter](../interpreter.md)): the events, including the Director's `attempt-retried` after a failed or stopped attempt ([decision](../decisions/0062-failed-attempt-retry.md)), what each records, the spec gate, derived mutation ids and replay after a crash between recording and launching an attempt.
Attempt preparation ([interpreter](../interpreter.md#attempt-preparation), [decision](../decisions/0036-immutable-attempt-preparation.md)): a prepared attempt is published once by renaming `prompt.partial` to `prompt`, replays launch from it unchanged after validating it against its manifest and recorded binding, and invalid preparation is refused with `ErrPreparation` without launching or removing anything.
Rebase-aware review ([interpreter](../interpreter.md#rebase-aware-review)) defines `Engine.Base`, the `Rebases` seam, the binding's `range` and `scope`, port `resolution`, and the revision-equality prerequisite for narrowing, and the full-review request a round consumes.
Validate ([interpreter](../interpreter.md#validate), [decision](../decisions/0066-validate-stage.md)): reading the forge's checks of the published head, the `checks` wait, the `guildhall.validation-result/1` result, the `failed` loop and its exit options, re-reading a waiting failed head and spending a round only on a failed check run the latest result did not hold, `Engine.ChecksRefresh` pacing with its `<step>.checks.json` read time, a `no_ci` repository, and `ErrValidationGate` before `deliver`.
Rounds and loops ([decision](../decisions/0035-loops-and-review-gate.md)): attempt bindings, the `previous`, `rulings` and `feedback` ports, blocking results, `max` with the `loop-exit` decision, stall messages, contested Director rulings and the dispute loop exit ([decision](../decisions/0040-director-rulings.md)), the review projection on outcome documents, `ErrOutcome` for a stale or undispositioned outcome and `ErrReviewGate` before `deliver`.
`Engine.Hold`, asked before each new attempt is recorded and before a recorded-but-unlaunched attempt is prepared or launched during recovery: a held advance records, prepares and launches nothing for it and waits `paused` with that step, the [Director's pause](../director.md#pause-and-resume).
The CLI supplies the store-backed hold reader and persists this wait in `next.json`; it has no pre-advance hold check.
Recovery of both task starts and retries uses this check for Artisan launches and Director-stage handoffs, and resumes the same recorded attempt after the hold lifts.
A hold-read error fails recovery without launching; results and already-launched attempts retain their normal handling.
`Engine.Caps.Hold` holds it where it is recorded: a start the records refuse with `store.ErrDirectorHeld` records and launches nothing and the advance waits `paused`, as when `Hold` holds it, so a pause between the two reads still holds the attempt ([decision](../decisions/0084-director-stop-admission.md)).
Publish takes an optional `spec` input, the accepted spec; its pull request title comes from the spec's heading (else the task's title), and its body, rewritten on every new head, has the sections Summary, What changed, How it was tested, Review and Guildhall (task, step, head and the accepted spec by name and digest, inlined), and for a task with `Fixes` ends with "Fixes a defect introduced by task `<repository>/<task>`." ([publish and deliver](../interpreter.md#publish-and-deliver)).
Deliver's audit ([publish and deliver](../interpreter.md#publish-and-deliver), [decision](../decisions/0087-quality-audit.md)): a merge of a task `config.Quality.Audits` samples opens decision `audit`, kind `audit`, bound to the head that merged, before closing the task, once even when the close fails and is advanced again, and sends it to the Director; the wait stays `done`.
Its consumer seams: `Records` (the store methods it calls, including `SetTaskState`, with which deliver closes a merged task, and `StartCappedAttempt`, with which every attempt starts under `Engine.Caps`), `Prompter` with `PromptFunc`, `Outbox` (the send half of `messaging.Mailbox`), `Forge` ([publish and deliver](../interpreter.md#publish-and-deliver) and [validate](../interpreter.md#validate), over [`internal/forge`](forge.md)), `Workspaces` ([the working directory](../interpreter.md#the-working-directory) and [the head check](../interpreter.md#the-head-check), over [`internal/git`](git.md#workspaces): `Ensure`, `Retire`, `Tip`, `IsAncestor` and `Base`) and the launch seam ([scripted mock agent](../mock-agent.md#the-launch-seam)): `Launcher`, `Spec` with its validation and `AdapterSpec`, whose `Copy` and `Profile` reference the stage's configuration and whose `Settings` are its profile's model and effective effort (resolved by [`config.EffectiveEffort`](config.md)), all carried unchanged onto the `adapter.Spec`, the `adapter.Spec` mapping the adapter conformance suite and `launch.Process` both use, `Handle`, and the `CheckAdapter` guard that refuses every adapter but `mock`, `fake` and a `claude` that resolves on `PATH` to the stub `ClaudeStubEnv` names inside a test binary.
Research uses its named profile through the same Artisan launch seam as plan and build, carrying the profile reference, adapter, model and effective effort to `Spec` and `AdapterSpec`.
`Engine.CheckSettings`, when set, is called after `CheckAdapter` with a profiled stage's adapter and settings, and its refusal fails the advance naming the profile before anything is recorded or launched; nil leaves the settings to the launch.
`BlockedQuestion` is the provisional file a blocked attempt writes.
The private history view ([history](../interpreter.md#history)) is a contract inside the package, not a seam: one parser edge for record names and the reads every step makes of rounds, exits, firings, feedback and outputs.

[Director stages](../interpreter.md#director-stages) ([decision](../decisions/0072-director-stages.md)): a `director` step is recorded, bound and prepared as an Artisan step is, then handed to the Director with a `director-stage` message (`store.MessageDirectorStage`) and a `launched.json` naming the director launcher (`HandedToDirector`), launching nothing, and waits with `WaitDirectorStage`; its result is taken as an Artisan's, and a published result not yet taken, which no launcher announces, is taken by any advance.

The [replay and recovery contract](../interpreter.md#replay-and-recovery) owns intake completion and concurrent outcome reconciliation.
The [intake-rejection decision](../decisions/intake-rejection.md) explains why rejection uses a distinct consumer-owned `Records.RejectAttempt` operation rather than another finish.

The [worker-stalled observation contract](../interpreter.md#worker-stalled-observations) defines the heartbeat file read, configured interval and clock, threshold crossings, replay identity and unchanged attempt state.

The [rounds and loops contract](../interpreter.md#rounds-and-loops) owns passing the recorded binding's revisions and range base into review preparation.
Required output examples are instructional, not an outcome recovery mechanism: missing review files and stale revisions are still refused.

## Refutation

The [plan-refutation contract](../interpreter.md#plan-refutation) owns document intake and acceptance semantics.
Refutation uses the existing Artisan `Launcher`, `Prompter` and `Records` seams; deterministic tests substitute a scripted launcher or fake runtime rather than calling a model.

`MergeabilityReader` is the narrow forge-observation seam for validate and deliver; GitHub and the forge double implement it, while legacy forges without it retain existing checks/refusal behaviour.
A conflicting or strict-behind PR that Guildhall cannot [rebase itself](../interpreter.md#tiered-rebase), a front that rebased three times in one stay or a copy that cannot rebase, records immutable evidence before sending a `pr-blocked` (or `rebase-stuck`) Director message, once per full head and state shared across steps, then waits for the Director.
The evidence carries PR URL, full head, base tip (the base branch's tip when measured), files, behind count and options.
Otherwise validate rebases a conflicting PR and reads a behind one's checks, and deliver queues the authorized merge and rebases only the front.
Validate retains the Director wait in its refresh cache; errors are not conflicts and unknown state does not stop the normal checks wait.

[The merge queue](../interpreter.md#the-merge-queue) and [tiered rebase](../interpreter.md#tiered-rebase) ([decision](../decisions/merge-queue-rebase.md)): `MergeQueue`, an optional `Records` extension that `*store.Store` and `cli.storeRecords` implement, with `MergeQueue`, `JoinMergeQueue`, `LeaveMergeQueue`, `ClaimMergeFront` and `ReleaseMergeFront`; records without it make every task its own queue's front.
Deliver authorizes before it queues, returns `WaitMergeQueue` behind the front, and at the front replays the head onto `Mergeability.Base` through `Workspaces.Rebase`, recording `<deliver>-<k>-rebase-<n>` (`guildhall.rebase/1`), `<deliver>-<k>-rebase-<n>-pr`, `<deliver>-<k>-checks-<head>` and, for a rebase it cannot stand, `<deliver>-<k>-escalated-<n>` (`guildhall.rebase-escalation/1`) with an automatic send-back of the build step.
A carried-over approval is `<deliver>-<k>-authorized`, `guildhall.merge-authorization/2`, with `carried_from`, `rebase`, `kind` and `spec_digest`; one already recorded for the rebased head is merged under again on replay or retry, never recorded twice.
Validate records `<validate>-<k>-rebase-<n>` for a conflicting PR, and its result is bound to the rebased head.
A rebase, a squash and every worktree move are refused while any attempt of the task runs, read afresh from `Records.Attempts`, and a front that already rebased three times in one stay waits for the Director instead of rebasing again.
A held front keeps the front until its own next advance releases it.
`mergequeue_test.go`, `mergequeue_engine_test.go` and `tier2_test.go` cover the queue, each tier at deliver and validate, every `reapprove_after_rebase` against every kind, the `never` carry-over across a major send-back, crash and replay before each record of tier 1, tier 2 and the validate rebase, the refusals while an attempt runs, hold, priority, rejoin, cancellation and the race for the front; the fixes after review 7 were run red first ([red runs](../evidence/merge-queue-review-7-2026-10-29-red.txt)).
The messages are `merge-queued`, `rebased`, `rebase-escalated`, `rebase-stuck` and `approval-carried`.
`StepSentBack` implements the [send-back admission, feedback and replay contract](../step-send-back.md); invalid requests and changed feedback are `ErrEvent`.
