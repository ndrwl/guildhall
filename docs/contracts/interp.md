# `internal/interp`

## Contract

`Engine.Advance`, the deterministic interpreter ([interpreter](../interpreter.md)): the events, including the Director's `attempt-retried` after a failed or stopped attempt ([decision](../decisions/0062-failed-attempt-retry.md)), what each records, the spec gate, derived mutation ids and replay after a crash between recording and launching an attempt.
Attempt preparation ([interpreter](../interpreter.md#attempt-preparation), [decision](../decisions/0036-immutable-attempt-preparation.md)): a prepared attempt is published once by renaming `prompt.partial` to `prompt`, replays launch from it unchanged after validating it against its manifest and recorded binding, and invalid preparation is refused with `ErrPreparation` without launching or removing anything.
Rebase-aware review ([interpreter](../interpreter.md#rebase-aware-review)) defines `Engine.Base`, the `Rebases` seam, the binding's `range` and `scope`, port `resolution`, and the revision-equality prerequisite for narrowing, and the full-review request a round consumes.
Validate ([interpreter](../interpreter.md#validate), [decision](../decisions/validate-stage.md)): reading the forge's checks of the published head, the `checks` wait, the `guildhall.validation-result/1` result, the `failed` loop and its exit options, re-reading a waiting failed head and spending a round only on a failed check run the latest result did not hold, `Engine.ChecksRefresh` pacing with its `<step>.checks.json` read time, a `no_ci` repository, and `ErrValidationGate` before `deliver`.
Rounds and loops ([decision](../decisions/0035-loops-and-review-gate.md)): attempt bindings, the `previous`, `rulings` and `feedback` ports, blocking results, `max` with the `loop-exit` decision, stall messages, contested Director rulings and the dispute loop exit ([decision](../decisions/0040-director-rulings.md)), the review projection on outcome documents, `ErrOutcome` for a stale or undispositioned outcome and `ErrReviewGate` before `deliver`.
Its consumer seams: `Records` (the store methods it calls, including `SetTaskState`, with which deliver closes a merged task), `Prompter` with `PromptFunc`, `Outbox` (the send half of `messaging.Mailbox`), `Forge` ([publish and deliver](../interpreter.md#publish-and-deliver) and [validate](../interpreter.md#validate), over [`internal/forge`](forge.md)), `Workspaces` ([the working directory](../interpreter.md#the-working-directory), over [`internal/git`](git.md#workspaces)) and the launch seam ([scripted mock agent](../mock-agent.md#the-launch-seam)): `Launcher`, `Spec` with its validation and `AdapterSpec`, the `adapter.Spec` mapping the adapter conformance suite and `launch.Process` both use, `Handle`, and the `CheckAdapter` guard that refuses every adapter but `mock`, `fake` and a `claude` that resolves on `PATH` to the stub `ClaudeStubEnv` names inside a test binary.
`BlockedQuestion` is the provisional file a blocked attempt writes.
The private history view ([history](../interpreter.md#history)) is a contract inside the package, not a seam: one parser edge for record names and the reads every step makes of rounds, exits, firings, feedback and outputs.

[Director stages](../interpreter.md#director-stages) ([decision](../decisions/director-stages.md)): a `director` step is recorded, bound and prepared as an Artisan step is, then handed to the Director with a `director-stage` message (`DirectorStage`) and a `launched.json` naming the director launcher (`HandedToDirector`), launching nothing, and waits with `WaitDirectorStage`; its result is taken as an Artisan's.

The [replay and recovery contract](../interpreter.md#replay-and-recovery) owns intake completion and concurrent outcome reconciliation.

The [worker-stalled observation contract](../interpreter.md#worker-stalled-observations) defines the heartbeat file read, configured interval and clock, threshold crossings, replay identity and unchanged attempt state.

`workspace_test.go` (new, red first against an engine that declared `Engine.Workspaces` but did not call it, [red run](../evidence/worktree-custody-2026-10-22-red.txt)): on both backends, a failed plan attempt and its retry each ensure the one workspace, a worktree of the repository on `guildhall/<task>` at `<state>/<repo>/<task>/work`, launch there and record the retry's output from there; a workspace that cannot be made fails the advance with nothing launched or marked launched, and the next advance launches the same attempt; without `Workspaces` the working directory stays a plain directory (passed on first run, pinning the existing behaviour).
Its retirement tests, red first against an engine that never called `Retire` ([red run](../evidence/worktree-custody-2026-10-22-red.txt)): on both backends a merge removes a workspace holding only recorded outputs and sends no `workspace-kept`, and keeps one holding another file, naming it in `workspace-kept`; a failing `Retire` is reported the same way; the task closes `done` in every case but an interrupt.
`TestInterruptedRetirementKeepsTheTaskOpen`, added with its fix during review and with no recorded red run, shows on both backends a `Retire` cut short by the advance's context ending fails the advance with the task open and no `workspace-kept`, and the next advance retires the workspace and closes the task.
