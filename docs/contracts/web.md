# `internal/web`

## Contract

`internal/web` serves the Guildmaster's task pages ([user doc](../web.md), [decision](../decisions/task-page.md)): one HTML page per task and a reader page per recorded document, laid out as d105's approved design-3, on which the Guildmaster's Tailscale login answers the task's open decisions or sends the Director words.
It renders and routes only, on `net/http`, `html/template` and `embed`; its one module is goldmark ([decision](../decisions/goldmark-for-documents.md)).
It never opens the store and never imports `internal/cli`.

### The seam

`Source` is the one interface: `Task(repo, id) (Task, error)` returns one task's page model, `ErrNotFound` for a task the copy does not hold and `ErrBusy` when a writer holds the store past the source's bounded wait; `Tasks() (TaskList, error)` returns the task list's model, or `ErrBusy`; `Tail(repo, id, after) (Tail, error)` returns only the tail of the task's conversation for the [new-reply bar](../web.md#the-new-reply-bar), its last message's number and the messages numbered above `after`, with `Task`'s errors.
Each call opens the store, reads and closes it before returning, so no page renders with the store open.
`webSource.Tail` is one read-only open with the page's bounded wait and `store.ConversationAfter`, which reads the task's record and the conversation's tail keys only; it builds none of Clerk's read, so a poll every few seconds holds the store's shared lock only for that read and a Director write after it takes the lock at once.
`TaskList` is its read time and one `Listed` row per task, open or closed in the last 7 days as Clerk's `/guild all` shows them, in the order the list shows each group's rows: repository, id, title, `Group` (`GroupYou`, `GroupMoving`, `GroupOther` or `GroupFinished`), the where-it-stands `Where` the task page shows, Clerk's phrase as its fallback, and since when.
`webSource.Tasks` reads only `clerkReadWait`'s status feed and views, no task's history: a task whose `Where` waits on the Guildmaster is `GroupYou`, since its oldest open ask; one waiting on an attempt, a slot, CI or the merge queue `GroupMoving`; any other open one `GroupOther`, each since its last receipt; and a closed one `GroupFinished`, since it closed. Open tasks come in Clerk's order, by priority then id, and finished ones newest first.
`Task` is plain values: title, state, Clerk's where-it-stands phrase and the page's own where-it-stands sentence (`Where`: a bold lead, the rest, and whether it waits on the Guildmaster), related tasks, the stepper's `Step`s, a multi-PR task's `Slices`, `Status` (step and round, latest attempt, latest pull request and its checks), the open and then deferred `Ask`s, the Director stage it waits on, its other needs_me reasons, the Guildmaster's words, its `Document`s (its own and other tasks' linked ones, each with path, sha256, group, step, round, recorded time and the label the page shows), its story `Node`s, oldest first, each in one `StepState`, titled by a sentence, with the record id it is about, its raw `Event`s and the `Tag` of the attempt or decision it is, its `Conversation`, the `Message`s of the task's [conversation](../web.md#the-conversation) (number, author, tag and the tag's stage, kind, Markdown text, time, and a recommendation's option and words or a confirmation's option and the number it confirms, or the round a round's answers answer), its `Drafts`, the latest saved draft of each question of a round not yet sent, and `Tag`, the current stage's latest attempt on an open task, with `TagStage`, its step; each `Ask` carries its `Stage` for its chip and a question's `Recommended` option.
The readable words are fixed templates in `internal/cli/web_words.go`, beside `docKind`: a step-type table (noun and finished-attempt sentence) and a decision-kind table (open, short phrase, answered by tone, and void sentences), which `TestWebWordsCoverEveryStepType` and `TestWebWordsCoverEveryDecisionKind` keep complete for every `workflow.Kinds()` type and decision kind; names come from `interp.RecordPlace`. Each friendly name keeps its record id in the element's `title`; Clerk's `stands` and its goldens are not changed.
`internal/cli`'s `webSource` fills it from Clerk's readers (`clerkReadWait`, `stands`, the status feed's decisions, needs_me and slices, `timeline` and `files`), so Clerk's own output is unchanged; the asks are the task's share of Clerk's Needs you split: its open decisions only, never an answered decision waiting for `task start`, a void decision or the copy's install item, and a `director-stage` wait is shown as the Director running that stage, not as an ask.
`internal/web` is tested with a fake `Source`; `webSource` against a real temporary store.

Two more interfaces answer, and with either missing, or `Options.Guildmaster` empty, every page is read-only and every answer refused:

- `Identifier`: `Login(ctx, remote)` returns the Tailscale login of the node at a request's remote `ip:port`, or an error matching `ErrNoIdentity`; an address that does not parse or lies outside the tailnet is refused without asking anyone. `Whois` is the real one: it runs `tailscale whois --json <remote>`, bounded at five seconds, and reads `UserProfile.LoginName`; a missing or failing `tailscale`, bad JSON or no login is `ErrNoIdentity`. `webtest.Logins` is the fake, and `webtest.RunIdentifier` the contract both pass.
- `Answerer`: `Answer(Answer)` records an option, with the words after a blank line, attributed to the page and the login, then messages and wakes the Director; an empty option answers an option-less decision by the words alone. `Send(Answer)` sends the words as a note, answering nothing, and returns its id, `NoteID`. `Answer` with `Confirm`, a recommendation's number, answers with that recommendation's option instead, refusing unless it is the Director's latest `recommendation` on the decision, and records a `confirmed` entry naming it with the answer. `Say(Answer)` sends the words about the attempt `Tag` as a note, as `Send` does, refusing a closed task or a tag that is no attempt or decision of the task, and records them under `Tag`. `Round(RoundPost)` saves every box of the open grill round as drafts, under a mutation naming the drafts saved before it, then marks a question accepted, answered with its words or open again, for Accept all recommendations (`RoundAcceptAll`) marks every unmarked question whose box is empty accepted, leaving any question with words, marked or not, as it is and sending nothing, or, for Send round with every question marked, records a `round-answers` entry and a `grill-answered` message, id `RoundID`, in one transaction and wakes the Director; Send round with a question unmarked refuses after saving, naming them, a round already sent replays Send round and refuses any other button, and a round not the latest of its running attempt, or on a closed task, is refused; the form posts `drafts`, the highest draft number it rendered of that round, and a press is refused with 409, writing nothing, when the round has a newer draft, unless it replays the same post. A `RoundPost` with `Plan`, a plan approval's open questions (`PlanQuestion`: number and recommended option), is a press of that approval's question controls instead: `Tag` is the decision, bound to `Subject`, and `Round` is `PlanRound`; it saves every box and the button's mark as the decision's drafts, refusing unless the decision is still open, bound to `Subject`, on an open task and the question is one of `Plan`'s, and sends nothing; it reads no `drafts`. `Answer` with `Plan` and `Boxes` saves the boxes as the decision's drafts first, while it is open, then follows the answer with `PlanAnswersText` of the drafts marked `accept` or `words`, nothing when none is, refusing an answer then over `store.MaxTextBytes` with the drafts kept. Each refuses with a `*Refusal` (an HTTP status and the reason the ask shows), returns `ErrBusy` for a held store, and writes nothing when it refuses. `internal/cli`'s `webAnswerer` implements it over `answerDecision`, `normaliseText`, the store mailbox, `surface` and `messaging.Wake`.

### Routes

`Server` routes `GET` and `HEAD`, and `POST` to three paths:

- `/`: the task list, read-only for every login, with no form;
- `/<repo>/<task>`: the task page, each ask anchored `#<decision-id>`;
- `/<repo>/<task>/doc/<name>`: the reader page of one of the task's documents;
- `/<repo>/<task>/doc/<name>/raw`: the document as `text/plain; charset=utf-8`;
- `/<repo>/<task>/doc/<name>/frame`: an HTML document only, as `text/html` with `FrameCSP`, `sandbox; default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; img-src data:; font-src data:; frame-src 'self' about:`, so it runs with no script, no forms and an opaque origin and loads nothing from the network, its only frames its own `srcdoc` ones, such as a design mock-up's desktop and phone frames; shown in an `<iframe sandbox>` on its reader page, and opened in a new tab from a design ask's "Open mock-up" link;
- `/<repo>/<task>/since?after=<n>`: the poll behind the new-reply bar, for the Guildmaster's login only (403 for any other, no identity or a read-only server), and refused 403 when the browser's `Sec-Fetch-Site` names another site (`cross-site` or `same-site`); an `after` that is not a number as `strconv.Itoa` writes it is 400, and none is 0. It reads `Source.Tail` alone and answers `application/json`, `{"start":…,"last":…,"new":…,"tag":…,"at":…}`: the server's per-process start id (`Server.start`, random and not the form token), the conversation's last number, how many messages after `after` are the Director's, and the newest one's tag and `15:04` time, both left out when there is none. It writes nothing, so it carries no token or `Origin` check;
- `POST /<repo>/<task>/answer`: a form with `decision`, `subject` (the digest the page showed, empty for none), `option`, `send` (`words` for words only, `answer` for an option-less decision's words), `text` and `token`, the server's per-process random form token, and, from a Confirm tile, `confirm`, the number of the recommendation it confirms, in place of `option`;
- `POST /<repo>/<task>/say`: the Say box, a form with `tag` (the current attempt), `text` and `token`;
- `POST /<repo>/<task>/round`: a button of the open grill round's form, with `tag` (its attempt), `round` (its conversation entry number), `drafts` (the highest draft number of the round the form rendered, 0 with none; a form with no `drafts` posts 0), `act` (`save`, `send`, `accept-all`, or `accept-<n>`, `words-<n>` or `change-<n>` for question n, at most `MaxRoundQuestions`), `text-<n>` for each box the form shows and `token`.

Every route first refuses, with a 421 refusal page and before reading anything, a request whose `Host` is not `Options.Host` (`internal/cli` sets it to the `[web]` address): the host part in any letter case, the port exactly, and a portless or trailing-dot `Host` refused; with no `Options.Host` every request is refused. So a DNS-rebinding page served under another name never sees a page or its token.
Without `Options.Host`, `Options.Identity`, `Options.Guildmaster` and `Options.Answerer` the server does not answer: every page is read-only and every `POST` refused.
Every `POST` passes one gate (`Server.gate`), in order: the server answers at all (403); `Identifier` names `Options.Guildmaster`'s login (403); its `Origin` is `http(s)://<Options.Host>`, matched as the `Host` is, never one taken from the request's `Host`, or, with no `Origin` or the opaque `null`, its `Sec-Fetch-Site` is `same-origin` (403); its token matches in constant time (403).
An answer is then accepted only when it names a decision and an option, a `send` or a positive `confirm` (400), and the `Answerer` takes it; words from the Say box only when they name a `tag` (400) and the `Answerer` takes them; a round's button only when it names a `tag`, a positive `round` and an `act`, and any `drafts` it posts is a non-negative integer (400), and the `Answerer`'s `Round` takes it: it saves every box as drafts first, so its refusals, such as Send round with a question unanswered, keep them, a round sent already replays Send round and refuses any other button, and a press from a page older than the round's newest draft is refused with 409, saving nothing ("changed since you loaded the page: its answers were saved from another tab or window"), unless it retries the same post. A round's refusal renders the page with the reason on the round and the posted boxes' words, or at the top of Now when the round is no longer open.
A round's button, Save, send later or Accept all recommendations, redirects to `#<tag>`, a question's to `#<tag>-q<n>` and Send round to `#<tag>-talk`.
A plan approval's question controls post to `/answer` with the approval's form: `act` (`save`, or `accept-<n>`, `words-<n>` or `change-<n>`) and `text-<n>` for each box; a form with `act` is a press, refused 400 for `send` or an act that does not read and 409 for an ask with no open questions on the page, given to `Round` with the spec's questions (`planQuestions`) and the changed picks that name one of their options other than the recommendation (`planPicks`, `RoundPost.Picks`), and redirected to `#<decision>-q<n>`, or `#<decision>` for Save. An answer with `approved`, `changes_requested` or a confirmation, not words only, on a plan approval carries the spec's questions, the boxes and those picks (`Answer.Picks`), and no "Qn (x)" lines; the spec is read only for such an answer, so other answers read nothing more than before. `internal/cli`'s `savePageDrafts` saves each such pick as its question's draft, marked `pick` with its letter, unless the press is that question's own button, which wins (the control touched last), and `PlanAnswersText` writes it once, "Q<n>: picked (b) <option>", with the box's words after a semicolon; a pick draft folds its card, "✓ Picked", and counts as answered. A refused press or answer renders the approval with the reason and the posted boxes' words.
A refusal writes nothing and renders the task page again with its status, the reason on the ask (class `refused`) and the words in its box, or for the Say box the reason and the words in the box; a refusal about a decision no longer among the asks, or from a Say box no longer on the page, shows at the top of Now.
An answer redirects 303 to `/<repo>/<task>#<decision>`, words only to `/<repo>/<task>?ask=<decision>&sent=<note id>#<decision>`, where the ask shows the words-only-sent state (class `sent`), and the Say box to `/<repo>/<task>?said=<note id>#<tag>-talk`, where the box shows its sent state.
The body is bounded at 1 MiB.

Each segment is path-escaped; an empty, `.` or `..` segment, a trailing slash, any other path and any other method is 404.
A document resolves only by a name the task's `Task` records for that task; its file is read from the record's slash path under `Options.Documents`, never from the URL, refused if the path climbs out, and served only when its sha256 matches the record.
A missing file is 404, a mismatch 409, and an unknown name 404, each a refusal page that names the document and never a filesystem path or an internal error.
A Markdown document (`.md`) is rendered by `RenderMarkdown`; an HTML one is never rendered inline; any other is shown as escaped text.
`ErrBusy` is 503 "The store is busy"; any other source error is a 500 with no detail, its error passed to `Options.Log`.

Every response carries `CSP` (`default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; img-src 'self'; frame-src 'self'; form-action 'self'; base-uri 'none'; frame-ancestors 'self'; script-src` followed by each own script's `'sha256-<base64>'` hash and nothing else, then `; connect-src 'self'`, the one request source, for `poll.js`) but the frame route's, refusals and the 421 wrong-host page included, with `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: same-origin` and `Cache-Control: no-store`; the policy is `same-origin`, not `no-referrer`, because under `no-referrer` a browser sends a form's `POST` with `Origin: null`.
A page is executed into a buffer first, so a template failure is a plain 500, not half a page.

### Pages

One embedded template (`page.tmpl`) and one stylesheet (`style.css`), inlined; only Guildhall's own scripts, no external request, `prefers-color-scheme` themes, the phone layout reflowed from the same markup at 760 px, and 44 px tap targets.
Only `RenderMarkdown`'s output, the embedded stylesheet and the own scripts reach the template unescaped; pull request URLs are linked only when they are `http` or `https`.

### Own scripts

An **own script** is a small, hand-written file under `internal/web/scripts/`, embedded in the binary; anything Artisans, pull requests or documents supply is **untrusted content** and never runs script ([decision](../decisions/own-scripts.md)).
Each own script is read once when the package loads, kept as `template.JS` with its `'sha256-<base64>'` hash computed from the same bytes, and inlined as an attribute-free `<script>` in the task list, task and reader pages; the refusal page and the mock-up frame carry none.
`CSP` is built once from those hashes: today's policy plus `script-src` and the hashes, never `'unsafe-inline'`, `'unsafe-eval'`, `'unsafe-hashes'`, `'strict-dynamic'`, `'self'`, a nonce or a host, then `connect-src 'self'`, so a script can ask only the page's own origin; with no own scripts it is exactly today's string. `FrameCSP` is unchanged.
There is no `.js` route, no `src=` script and no nonce.
The first own script, `js.js`, adds the class `js` to `<html>`, a hook for styles that apply only when scripts run; no style uses it yet.
The second, `poll.js`, drives the [new-reply bar](../web.md#the-new-reply-bar): on a page with the bar (`#newbar`, only the Guildmaster's login's page of an open task, drawn hidden by the `newbar` template with `data-since`, `data-base`, `data-last` and `data-start`), it GETs `data-since?after=<data-last>` every 5 s while the tab is visible and every 60 s while it is hidden, and at once when the tab becomes visible again. When `new` is above 0 it fills the bar's tag and time with `textContent`, sets Reload's `href` to `<data-base>?seen=<last>#<tag>-talk`, so following it loads the page anew at the talk, shows the bar and prefixes the tab's title with `(<new>) `; when `start` is not `data-start` it shows the line saying the page is out of date. It never reloads the page or submits anything; a failed poll changes nothing and the next one is still scheduled.

Own scripts follow these rules, which `TestOwnScriptCodeRules` checks:

- No template action inside `<script>`: pages pass data to scripts only through existing DOM elements and `data-*` attributes.
- No `innerHTML`, `outerHTML`, `insertAdjacentHTML`, `document.write`, `eval`, `new Function`, string `setTimeout` or `setInterval`, `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `WebSocket`, `EventSource`, `sendBeacon`, `import(` or setting or reloading `location`, except `poll.js`'s one `fetch`, a same-origin `GET` of its bar's `data-since`, written exactly as the test pins it.
- Listeners are attached only with `addEventListener`, never with an `on…=` attribute in `page.tmpl` or an `on…` property.
- Scripts never submit anything; only the Guildmaster's click submits.
- Every page works with scripts turned off: scripts only enhance it, and the server keeps every check it makes today.
For the Guildmaster's login every ask, deferred ones too, is a form: the Your words box, one submit tile per option, Send words only under a dashed line, and "Answering as <login> · your Tailscale login"; the reader page's asks are the same forms.
For any other login or no identity every ask is read-only: disabled tiles, no form, no box, and "Only the Guildmaster's Tailscale login can answer here", naming the login when there is one.
The task list's band carries the count line, such as "3 tasks wait for you · 5 are moving · 3 wait on something else" (`countLine`), amber when a task waits for the Guildmaster; then come the groups that hold a task, Waiting for you (amber bar), Moving and Waiting on something else, and last the fold "Finished in the last 7 days · n". Each row is the task's id, `<repo>/<id>` when the list spans more than one repository, linked to its page with a 44 px target, its title, its where-it-stands sentence and since when. The task page's crumb starts with "All tasks", linked to `/`.
The band shows the where-it-stands sentence under the title, then in grey the slice, pull request and CI, falling back to Clerk's phrase when `Where` is empty; the phone's chips are the pull request and CI only. Status's first row is "Where it stands" and its attempt row "Last attempt", the attempt's name titled with its record id.
The story shows, for an open task, one hollow node saying which of the workflow's steps come next, then its six newest nodes, then folds the rest under "N earlier entries"; each node with raw events folds them under "Raw events · n", one `15:04 <record>` line each.
The Needs you index names each open ask by `Ask.Phrase`, capitalised, or, when the Source gives none, by its kind and the document its subject matches or else its short subject digest or else its options, and its `#id`.
An ask's headline (`<p class="q">`) is `Ask.Headline` when the Source gives one, its story sentence from `webAskWords`, the one per-kind table of ask words beside the option meanings, which `TestWebWordsCoverEveryDecisionKind` keeps complete; the question's first line then leads "The rest of the question". With no headline the question's first line is the headline, as before.
Under the headline, before any summary, questions or subject line, the `explanation` template draws the Director's explanation (`Ask.Explanation`, from the status feed's `decisions[].explanation`): "From the Director · <time>" and its text through `RenderMarkdown` only, in a block of its own (`.dx`); with none, the grey note "The Director has not explained this ask yet.", and with a stale one (`Explanation.Stale`, its subject digest no longer the decision's), "The Director's explanation was written for an earlier version of this ask, so it is not shown.", the stale text never shown; each note goes on to point at "The rest of the question" when the ask has one. The explanation marks no tile itself: its recommendation reaches the page as the ask's one `Ask.Recommendation`.
The rail's "What the grill decided" fold is the newest grill notes document's `## Decisions` section rendered by goldmark, counted by its top-level list items, or a link to the document when it has none or its file does not verify.
A plan approval ask (kind `approval` whose subject document is one of the task's documents of kind `Spec`) shows, unfolded under its question's first line, the spec's `## Summary` block (`spec.Summary`, from `internal/spec`) read through the same verified read and rendered by goldmark under "Summary", or the link "No summary in this spec · read <spec>" when the spec has none or its file does not verify, in place of the subject line's own link; other approvals, such as a design round's about its design document, show neither. After the block, under a rule, or after the fallback link, it shows the spec's open questions (`spec.ReadSpecQuestions`, the parse the question text's `spec.PlanPart` comes from) under "Open questions" with a count badge, one card each, or "Open questions: none."; a spec with no readable section shows no part. On a page that answers, the cards are the approval's question controls (`planDrafts`, from `Task.Drafts` under the decision and `PlanRound`): the heading is "Open questions · <answered> of <n> answered", each card (`#<decision>-q<n>`) folds as an open round's question does once marked, or shows its box, "saved <time> · not an answer yet", Accept recommendation when it has a lettered recommendation and Answer with my words, and the cards end with Save, answer later; with a question answered, Approved reads `PlanTileNote`, such as "with your 2 answers; Q3 and Q4 take the recommendation", and Changes requested "send it back" with it.
A card (`questions.go`'s `newQuestionsView`, drawn by the `questions` template) has a `Qn` chip and the title, the body, and the options as rows: the recommended option first, whatever its letter, with a green tint, a green left bar, its letter in green, a **Recommended** badge and the `Recommended:` line's reason on a smaller line beneath, then the others in letter order; letters are kept as written. A hallmark question's rows are its recommended answer, marked the same way, and its `Alternative:`. A question that does not read into parts (`spec.Question.Shaped`), such as one with a free-text `Recommended:` line in a spec recorded before the letter rule, shows its lines word for word with no marker. Option text, bodies and reasons are inline Markdown, each rendered by goldmark at its safe defaults; titles are escaped. Cards are cut where the question text's part is, at `spec.QuestionsMax`. On a phone every card and row stacks in one column, with nothing scrolling sideways.
When a plan or design approval shows at least one card with a marked option, its answer row (`answerTiles`, drawn by the `answers` template) puts **Approved** first, spanning the row, tinted green with a **Recommended** badge and "take every recommendation above; the task goes on"; **Changes requested** reads "send it back with your words, such as “Q2 (a)”", and the Your words hint says to name any answer to change. The option values do not change, and the option-meanings table is not changed for it. Asks with no such card, and a follow-ups ask's pick and none, keep their tiles.
On the Guildmaster's form, such an ask's lettered cards (`cardView.Pick`, set by `questionsView.withPicks`), a plan approval's open cards beside their controls included, are radio groups, one per card, named `pick-<N>`: each row is a `<label>`, at least 44 px tall, wrapping its radio, whose id is `p-<ask>-<N>-<letter>` (`newQuestionsView` takes the ask id) and whose value is the row's bare letter (`rowView.Letter`), except the recommended row's, which is empty and checked. Each other row carries a hidden "Your answer: Qn → (x)" line, shown by CSS while its radio is checked; while any pick on the ask is changed, CSS `:has()` hides Approved and marks Changes requested, except on a plan approval's controls (`.oqs.ctl`), where Approved stays and both tiles show their `tileView.Picked` line (`PlanPickedNote`) in place of their own. The read-only page keeps the static rows, with no inputs. The POST (`answer.go`'s `readPicks`) refuses, before the `Answerer`, any `pick-<N>` field whose N is not a positive number as written by `strconv.Itoa`, whose value is not empty or one lower-case letter, or that is sent twice, with "a pick is malformed"; it does not read the document. Changed picks become "Qn (x)" lines in question order (`withPicks`), then a blank line and the words, or the lines alone for blank words, on `changes_requested` and on words only; `approved` with a changed pick is refused, "you changed Qn; choose Changes requested"; any other answer and a confirmation ignore them. On a plan approval with open questions on the page (`planQuestions`), a press, `approved`, `changes_requested` and a confirmation carry them as its questions' answers instead, as above, so `approved` is not refused for them; words only keeps the lines. A refusal keeps the picks (`pageState.Picks`) and the cards check them again.
The `questions` and `answers` templates and their Go builders are the one layout of an ask's questions and answers: every ask that lays out questions and answers, such as d150's, calls them rather than a layout of its own, so a change to one changes all.
The question's own Summary copy (`**Summary**`, or `Plan summary:` in an ask recorded before d153), when the page shows the block, its `No summary…` line and its open-questions part, in either shape (`**Open questions · n**` or the older `Open questions in full:`), when the page shows one, are taken out of "The rest of the question", so each shows once; an older ask's copy, which differs from the page's, is cut up to the question's next part.
The page finds a question's parts only by the markers `spec` names for them (`SummaryHeader` and the older `OldSummaryHeader`, `NoSummaryLine`, `InstructionsHeader`, `InputsHeader`, `AnswerLine` and `DesignAnswersLine`), the ones `render` writes; `web` keeps no copy of `render`'s wording and does not import `render`.
A design round's ask (kind `approval` whose subject document is of kind `Design`) shows no plan summary, but, unfolded under its question's first line, its design's open questions (`spec.ReadSpecQuestions` on the verified design, the parse the part `render.DesignQuestion` writes comes from) as cards in a box of their own; the question's own copy is taken out of "The rest of the question". A design with no readable section, or whose file does not verify, shows no box.
A hallmark's follow-ups ask (kind `follow-ups`, whose subject document is its report) shows, unfolded under its question's first line, the report's `## Questions` (`spec.ReportQuestions` on the verified report, the parse the part the follow-ups question carries after its ranked list comes from) as cards in a box of their own; the question's own copy, whole, cut or as stored, in either shape, is taken out of "The rest of the question" from its heading up to the answer format, so the ranked list and the answer format stay there. A report with no readable section, or whose file does not verify, shows no box and keeps its question whole.
A design ask's `Ask.Mockup` names its mock-up, which `webSource` fills for an approval whose subject document is a designer attempt's design, `designer-<k>-design`, with the mock-up `interp.MockupDocument` names among the task's documents: that attempt's named `mockup` part, `designer-<k>-mockup`, or for a mock-up recorded before that part, its legacy name `designer-<k>-design-2`. An HTML mock-up adds "Open mock-up" to the subject line, a link to its frame route that opens in a new tab, so its frames get the full window width; it is never framed or inlined in the task page. A Markdown mock-up is read through the verified read and rendered by goldmark under "Mock-up", unfolded under the subject line, so its fenced sample output shows as preformatted text; one whose file does not verify, or of any other type, is a link to its reader page instead. A design ask with no mock-up shows neither.
The conversation's messages are ledger rows (class `msg`, `dir` for the Director, `wait` for a `recommendation` whose ask is open), each with its author (You on the Guildmaster's login's page, else Guildmaster, with `page` or `chat`, or Director), its time and its body rendered by `RenderMarkdown`. A message shows once, by its tag: inside its open ask, after the question, summary, mock-up and findings and before Your words, under "Talk on #<id>" at `#<id>-talk`; under the asks, as the current stage's talk on `Task.Tag` with its newest three shown and the rest folded, on an open task; folded shut under its story node (anchored `#story-<tag>`) as "Talk · n messages"; or, when its tag has none of these, under the asks as its own talk. Each ask's header has a chip, `<stage> · <id>`, linking to its talk, or else its story node, or else the ask; a story node whose talk is on Now says where. The rail's Status adds "Talk · n messages · last <time>" when there is any, and a done or cancelled task with talk says on Now that it is closed and its talk stays read-only.
A recommendation's head says "recommends <answer>"; a `confirmed` entry's row (class `conf`) says "↩ Confirmed: <answer>" over the words its answer carried. An ask's one recommendation is `Ask.Recommendation` (`Option`, `Words`, `Number`, `At`, `Stale`), which `webSource` fills from the decision's [recommendation record](../store.md#decision-recommendations), `Stale` when its digest is not the decision's; a decision with no record, opened before records were kept, falls back to its question's `recommended` field and then to its current explanation's `recommend`, both with `Number` 0. The page reads nothing else for it. A current one whose option the ask offers sets the `Rec` flag on that tile, moved first (`markRecommended`, the one function that marks a tile), unless the ask marks its own, as a plan or design approval's Approved is; a stale one marks no tile and adds the line (class `recst`) "The Director recommended <answer> against an earlier version; it is stale." A current one with `Number` above 0, from `task say`, whose option the ask offers, adds an amber box "Recommended by the Director" with one tile, "Confirm: <answer>", a submit of the ask's form named `confirm` with value `Number` (disabled on a read-only page), after the ask's talk and before Your words and the tiles; the words box is then pre-filled with its `Words`, or else the Guildmaster's latest words on the ask before that entry. On POST, `webRecommendation` reads the record with the answer: a `confirm` that is no Director recommendation entry on the decision is 400, one that is not the record's `Number` is 409 "the Director has recommended again", and a stale record is 409 "the Director's recommendation on #<id> is stale; reload and read it", each writing nothing; otherwise the answer is the record's option.
On an open task with a `Task.Tag`, the Guildmaster's login's page ends the current stage's talk with the Say box (`#say`), "Say to the Director about <tag>" and **Send to the Director**, posting to `/say`; with no talk on the stage yet it stands alone under a "Talk on <chip>" heading. No other page has it, and a closed task renders no form.
A `round` message's text is a grill round (`ParseRound`: `**Q<n>. <title>**` heads numbered from 1, each a body and a last line `Recommended: <answer>`, at most `MaxRoundQuestions`), and a `round-answers` message (`RoundAnswersText`, read back by `ParseRoundAnswers`) answers the round its `Round` names. The latest round on `Task.Tag` of an open task with no answers is the open round: a card (class `ask round`, anchored `#<tag>`) after the asks, "Grill round <k>" with its progress, one form posting to `/round` on the Guildmaster's login's page, carrying `drafts`, the highest `Task.Drafts` `Number` of that round, or 0 with none, with no form for anyone else. Each question (`#<tag>-q<n>`) shows its title, body and recommendation; one whose latest `Task.Drafts` entry is marked `accept` or `words` folds to "✓ Accepted" or "✓ Your words, saved" with its gist and a Change button, and any other shows its box, holding the draft's words, "saved <time> · not an answer yet" when it has words, and Accept recommendation and Answer with my words; the card ends with a sticky bar of Save, send later, Accept all recommendations ("accepts every question with an empty box; your words are kept", only while some unmarked question's saved box is empty) and Send round, "<n> still to answer". The rail's Needs you lists it, "Grill round <k> · <answered> of <n>", and counts it. Every other round folds into one Director row (`#<tag>-round-<k>`), "Round <k> · <n> questions · sent <time> · <tally>" opening to each question and its answer, or "⊘ … not sent", with "its saved answers were dropped" on a closed task; a `round-answers` message has no row of its own.

### Rendering, options and binding

- `RenderMarkdown` is goldmark with its tables extension at its safe defaults: raw HTML omitted, and a dangerous URL blanked, the `<a>` or `<img>` kept with an empty `href` or `src`. The dangerous URLs are `javascript:`, `vbscript:`, `file:` and every `data:` URL except `data:image/png`, `gif`, `jpeg` or `webp` followed by `;base64,`, matched case-insensitively; those four base64 images are kept, and the page's CSP (`img-src 'self'`) stops the browser loading them. `TestMarkdownDataURLs` pins the rule.
- `OptionMeaning(kind, option)` reads the option-meanings table: a one-line meaning and a `Tone` (positive, negative, carry on) for every option of every set `interp.DecisionOptions`, `render.DecisionOptions`, `store.DecisionOptions`, `cli.StewardDecisionOptions` and `admit.AlertDecisionOptions` export; an option it does not describe shows its word alone, neutral.
- `BindAddress(ctx, address, lookup)` resolves a `[web]` address and returns its first address with the port, refusing an address with no host or port, a host that does not resolve, and with `ErrNotTailnet` a host any of whose addresses lies outside `100.64.0.0/10` and `fd7a:115c:a1e0::/48`; `Listen` binds what it returns. `URL(address, repo, task)` is a task page's link.
- `Serve(ln, h)` serves with bounded header, read, write and idle times; `Running.Close` lets requests finish for up to five seconds, then closes them, and returns once stopped.
