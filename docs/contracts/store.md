# `internal/store`

## Contract

`Open`, locking, mutation ids and receipts, and the records, messages, inboxes and workflow approvals (`docs/store.md`, D9, D10, D15, D22), including typed decisions bound to a subject with option-checked answers (D23) and the `audit` kind, which needs a subject like every kind but `question` ([decision](../decisions/0087-quality-audit.md)), Director rulings recorded once and contested once with the review projection on document references ([director-rulings](../decisions/0040-director-rulings.md)), full-review requests requested and consumed once ([full review](../tasks.md#full-review)), and receipts naming their record with `TaskHistory`'s one-transaction task read (D24) and `DirectorView`'s one-transaction read of the whole store for [`director status`](../director.md#status).
Attempt ownership ([store-attempt-ownership](../decisions/0031-store-attempt-ownership.md)): `Ownership`, `StartOwnedAttempt`, `TakeOwnership` with its required `OwnerProbe` seam, `ProbeLauncher` over a caller-supplied `IdentityProbe`, the store-neutral `ProcessIdentity` and `Liveness` values and `ErrOwnerLive`/`ErrOwnerUnknown` refusals, `UpdateOwnership`, `Ownership`, and release by `FinishAttempt`; `StopUnownedAttempt` records an attempt with no unreleased owner `stopped` in one mutation, refusing an owned one with `ErrOwned`, a finished one with `ErrInvalid` and a missing one with `ErrNotFound`, and its finish-attempt receipt lets a later equal `FinishAttempt` stand.
The ownership record's `Containment` is a recovery description of every Artisan containment kind ([containment-persistence](../decisions/0037-containment-persistence.md)): `Containment.Recoverable` and `ErrUnrecoverable`, the kind constants, a worker identity in place of a name for process groups, the Linux detection reason, and legacy records read unchanged but never recoverable when incomplete.

Director sessions and wakes in the `director` bucket ([director-session-record](../decisions/0067-director-session-record.md)): `StartDirectorSession` numbered from 1 and superseding every earlier session that has not stopped, with the store-set `EndReason` `EndSuperseded`, `SetDirectorState`'s forward-only moves with `running` and `paused` moving to each other and a compare-and-set on `StateSequence`, `RequestDirectorStop` to `stopping` with `StopNow` and `Sweeping`, `EndDirectorSession` to `stopped` with an end reason and `FinishDirectorStop` clearing `Sweeping`, a stopped session's included, under the same rules ([director-pause-stop](../decisions/0073-director-pause-stop.md)), `DirectorSession` and `LatestDirectorSession`; `RecordWake` once per message to the Director with `WakeFor` and ordered, bounded `Wakes`; `DirectorUnread`'s counts by type, keyed only by `MessageTypes`, the registry of the types Guildhall sends with each one's sender, and `other` for every other type, which the [wake](../messaging.md#waking-the-director) types; `DirectorView.Session`, the newest session, and `DirectorView.LastWake`, the newest wake, read in the view's transaction; and `Doctor`'s `DirectorSessions` and `Wakes`, with doctor decoding every session, wake, handoff note, reload request and handoff due and reporting one that does not decode as a problem.
Handoff notes and reload requests in the same bucket ([director-handoff-reload](../decisions/0082-director-handoff-reload.md)): `RecordHandoffNote` for the newest live session, keyed on the session, the digest and the newest note before it so only a repeat in a row replays, the store choosing `HandoffPath` from the receipt sequence and calling the caller's write before committing, with `LatestHandoffNote`; `RequestDirectorReload`, which collapses requests while one is `requested` and upgrades it for `--now`, `ApplyDirectorReload` with the old and new sessions, and `LatestDirectorReload`; and `DirectorView.Handoff` and `DirectorView.Reload`.
Handoff dues in the same bucket ([director-handoff-triggers](../decisions/0085-director-handoff-triggers.md)): a session's `Handoff` policy validated when it starts; `RecordHandoffDue` keyed on the session, trigger and crossing so each crossing is recorded once, refusing an unknown session, trigger, a crossing below 1 and a reason that is blank, multi-line or too long; `HandoffDues` per session in order; and `FinishedTasks`, the task state receipts after the session's start receipt.

Attempt caps ([guard rails](../decisions/0074-guard-rails.md)): `StartCappedAttempt` counts running attempts and the current UTC day's starts in the start's own mutation and refuses at a cap with a `*CapRefusal` wrapping `ErrAttemptCap`, writing nothing; a replay returns its receipt whatever the caps; `StartAttempt` is it with no caps; `AttemptUsage` and `DirectorView.Usage` read the same counts ([attempts](../store.md#attempts)).
`CountUsage` is the one rule for what the caps count, given the receipts and attempts of a store at an instant: running attempts across every repository, `start-attempt` and `start-owned-attempt` receipts committed since the instant's UTC day began, and the spend of the attempts those receipts started; `StartCappedAttempt`, `AttemptUsage` and the records double call it, and the store's day is its `Options.Now`.
`AttemptCaps.Hold` refuses the start with `ErrDirectorHeld`, writing nothing, while the newest Director session `Holds`: it is `paused` or `stopping`, or `Sweeping`, read in the same mutation, so no start commits after a pause or stop has until the stop's sweep finishes ([decision](../decisions/0084-director-stop-admission.md)).
Attempt spend ([attempt spend](../decisions/0079-attempt-spend.md)): `StageAttempt.Spend`, a `Spend` of tokens and a US dollar cost each with a `Measure` (`SpendMeasured`, `SpendEstimated`, `SpendUnknown`), recorded by `RecordAttemptSpend` on a running or finished attempt, refused with `ErrInvalid` when invalid, the first record standing and a different one refused ([attempts](../store.md#attempts)); `SpendTotal.AddAttempt` from `NoSpend` is the one rule for totals, and `AttemptUsage.Spend` totals the attempts started on its day.
`DirectorUnread` counts `budget-refused` and `advance-failed` by name; the `advance-failed` count in `TestDirectorUnreadCountsByType` was red against the list without it ([red run](../evidence/watcher-advance-and-wake-2026-10-22-red.txt)).

`SkipSyncForTests` makes every store a test binary opens afterwards skip bbolt's fsync of each commit ([windows-ci-speed](../decisions/0081-windows-ci-speed.md)); records, receipts, locking and replay are unchanged and only a power loss could lose a commit.
It panics outside a test binary (`testing.Testing`), so production stores sync every commit.
The test binaries of `internal/store`, `messaging`, `interp`, `cli`, `launch`, `artisan` and `reconcile` call it from `TestMain`, which their own child processes run too; `cmd/guildhall`'s built binary syncs as production does.

The [attempt ownership contract](../store.md#attempt-ownership) defines monotonic recovery evidence, `ProbeLauncher`'s containment refusal and the explicit containment-aware recovery path.
