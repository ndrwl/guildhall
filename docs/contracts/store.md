# `internal/store`

## Contract

`ContainmentNotStarted` records explicit evidence that completed supervision never invoked worker startup.
It is recoverable only with no name, reason or worker identity; it is not inferred from an empty record.
`TestContainmentRoundTrips` and `TestContainmentRequiresRecoveryEvidence` cover its persistence and inconsistent-evidence refusals.

`Open`, locking, mutation ids and receipts, and the records, messages, inboxes and workflow approvals (`docs/store.md`; decisions [bbolt](../decisions/bbolt-for-the-store.md), [single writer](../decisions/store-single-writer-lock.md), [messaging](../decisions/local-messaging-in-the-store.md) and [workflow approvals](../decisions/workflow-approvals.md)), including typed decisions bound to a subject with option-checked answers ([decision](../decisions/typed-decisions-bound-to-an-artefact.md)) and the `audit` kind, which needs a subject like every kind but `question` ([decision](../decisions/retire-quality-audit.md)), Director rulings recorded once and contested once with the review projection on document references ([director-rulings](../decisions/director-rulings.md)), full-review requests requested and consumed once ([full review](../tasks.md#full-review)), attempt parks in the lazily created `attempt-parks` bucket, `ParkAttempt` refusing in its transaction what cannot be parked and a cycle other than the caller's, `UnparkAttempt` keeping the record with the next cycle, and `AttemptPark` ([decision](../decisions/park-attempt.md); `park_test.go`, run red against a stub that parked anything and unparked without checks, [evidence](../evidence/park-attempt-2026-10-29-red.txt)), task grants in the lazily created `task-grants` bucket, `CreateTaskGrant` checking every rule with `CheckTaskGrantRule`, the step against the claude steps the caller passes, `all` needing `until = done` and one pending or active grant per task and step, and opening its `grant-<id>` decision of the subject-less `grant` kind in the same transaction, `RevokeTaskGrant` recording one revoke, `BindTaskGrant` recording an attempt that took an active grant, once only for `until = attempt`, `TaskGrant.State` deriving its state and `TaskGrants` and `TaskGrantStatuses` reading them, the latter with each approval's commit time ([decision](../decisions/task-grants.md); `grants_test.go`), task priorities with their history ([priority](../tasks.md#priority)), the per-repository merge queue and merge holds ([merge queue](../interpreter.md#the-merge-queue)), and receipts naming their record with `TaskHistory`'s one-transaction task read ([decision](../decisions/inspection-reads-from-store-receipts.md)) and `DirectorView`'s one-transaction read of the whole store for [`director status`](../director.md#status).
Attempt ownership ([store-attempt-ownership](../decisions/store-attempt-ownership.md)): `Ownership`, `StartOwnedAttempt`, `TakeOwnership` with its required `OwnerProbe` seam, `ProbeLauncher` over a caller-supplied `IdentityProbe`, the store-neutral `ProcessIdentity` and `Liveness` values and `ErrOwnerLive`/`ErrOwnerUnknown` refusals, `UpdateOwnership`, `Ownership`, and release by `FinishAttempt`; `StopUnownedAttempt` records an attempt with no unreleased owner `stopped` in one mutation, refusing an owned one with `ErrOwned`, a finished one with `ErrInvalid` and a missing one with `ErrNotFound`, and its finish-attempt receipt lets a later equal `FinishAttempt` stand.
The ownership record's `Containment` is a recovery description of every Artisan containment kind ([containment-persistence](../decisions/containment-persistence.md)): `Containment.Recoverable` and `ErrUnrecoverable`, the kind constants, a worker identity in place of a name for process groups, the Linux detection reason, and legacy records read unchanged but never recoverable when incomplete.

Director sessions and wakes in the `director` bucket ([director-session-record](../decisions/director-start.md)): `StartDirectorSession` numbered from 1 and superseding every earlier session that has not stopped, with the store-set `EndReason` `EndSuperseded`, `SetDirectorState`'s forward-only moves with `running` and `paused` moving to each other and a compare-and-set on `StateSequence`, `RequestDirectorStop` to `stopping` with `StopNow` and `Sweeping`, or setting only `Sweeping` on a stopped, non-sweeping session with a recorded runtime for recovery, `EndDirectorSession` to `stopped` with an end reason and `FinishDirectorStop` clearing `Sweeping`, a stopped session's included, under the same rules ([director-pause-stop](../decisions/director-pause-stop.md)), `RecordDirectorRuntime` recording the runtime's recoverable `Containment` once per session under `director-runtime:<n>` without moving `StateSequence`, refused for a session not the newest or `stopped` ([director-runtime-record](../decisions/director-runtime-record.md)), `DirectorSession` and `LatestDirectorSession`; `RecordWake` once per message to the Director with `WakeFor` and ordered, bounded `Wakes`; `DirectorUnread`'s counts by type, keyed only by `MessageTypes`, the registry of the types Guildhall sends with each one's sender, and `other` for every other type, which the [wake](../messaging.md#waking-the-director) types; `DirectorView.Session`, the newest session, and `DirectorView.LastWake`, the newest wake, read in the view's transaction; and `Doctor`'s `DirectorSessions` and `Wakes`, with doctor decoding every session, wake, handoff note, note, note version, reload request and handoff due and reporting one that does not decode as a problem.
Reload requests in the same bucket ([director-handoff-reload](../decisions/director-reload-and-notes.md)): `RequestDirectorReload`, which collapses requests while one is `requested` and upgrades it for `--now`, `ApplyDirectorReload` with the old and new sessions, and `LatestDirectorReload`; and `DirectorView.Reload`.
The retired handoff notes ([D94](../decisions/director-reload-and-notes.md)) keep only `HandoffNote` and the `handoffs` bucket, so `Doctor` still decodes the records stores hold; `RecordHandoffNote`, `LatestHandoffNote` and `DirectorView.Handoff` are gone.
Director notes in the same bucket ([store](../store.md#director-notes)): `AddNote`, `EditNote` and `RemoveNote` for the newest live session, checked in the transaction as a handoff note is, each recording a `NoteVersion` keyed by its receipt sequence and keeping the note's `Version`, ids `n<k>` from the `notes` bucket's own sequence, the kind, one-line text, reason and task refusals, an edit keyed on the version it follows and refused with `ErrExists` when the note changed since, a removed note kept and refused another edit or remove; `LiveNotes` by kind and id; and `NoteVersions`.
Handoff dues in the same bucket ([director-handoff-triggers](../decisions/director-handoff-triggers.md)): a session's `Handoff` policy validated when it starts; `RecordHandoffDue` keyed on the session, trigger and crossing so each crossing is recorded once, refusing an unknown session, trigger, a crossing below 1 and a reason that is blank, multi-line or too long; `HandoffDues` per session in order; and `FinishedTasks`, the task state and task cancel receipts after the session's start receipt.
[Task cancel](../tasks.md#task-cancel)'s writes ([decision](../decisions/task-cancel.md)): `CancelTask` cancels an open task with a non-blank reason and the commit time, refusing a closed task with `ErrInvalid` and an unknown one with `ErrNotFound`, and `VoidDecision` voids an unanswered decision, replaying a same-reasoned void under another id and refusing another reason or an answered decision with `ErrInvalid`; `AnswerDecision` refuses a void decision, and `DirectorView.OpenDecisions` leaves it out. Both run in the shared records contract against the store, `cli.storeRecords` and the double, after the implementation, with a [failure check](../evidence/task-cancel-2026-10-29-red.txt) against stubs.

`StartAttempt`, `StartCappedAttempt` and `StartOwnedAttempt` start an attempt on an open task only, except an attempt of `workflow.JudgeStep`, which starts on a `done` task only ([judge](../decisions/quality-rubric.md)); `CreateTask` keeps a task's optional `Fixes`, the earlier task whose defect it fixes, which must exist and cannot be the task itself.
Attempt caps ([guard rails](../decisions/guard-rails.md)): `StartCappedAttempt` counts running attempts and the current UTC day's starts in the start's own mutation and refuses at a cap with a `*CapRefusal` wrapping `ErrAttemptCap`, writing nothing; a replay returns its receipt whatever the caps; `StartAttempt` is it with no caps; `AttemptUsage` and `DirectorView.Usage` read the same counts ([attempts](../store.md#attempts)).
`CountUsage` is the one rule for what the caps count, given the receipts and attempts of a store at an instant: running attempts across every repository, `start-attempt` and `start-owned-attempt` receipts committed since the instant's UTC day began, and the spend of the attempts those receipts started; `StartCappedAttempt`, `AttemptUsage` and the records double call it, and the store's day is its `Options.Now`.
`AttemptCaps.Hold` refuses the start with `ErrDirectorHeld`, writing nothing, while the newest Director session `Holds`: it is `paused` or `stopping`, or `Sweeping`, read in the same mutation, so no start commits after a pause or stop has until the stop's sweep finishes ([decision](../decisions/director-pause-stop.md)).
Attempt spend ([attempt spend](../decisions/attempt-spend.md)): `StageAttempt.Spend`, a `Spend` of tokens and a US dollar cost each with a `Measure` (`SpendMeasured`, `SpendEstimated`, `SpendUnknown`), recorded by `RecordAttemptSpend` on a running or finished attempt, refused with `ErrInvalid` when invalid, the first record standing and a different one refused ([attempts](../store.md#attempts)); `SpendTotal.AddAttempt` from `NoSpend` is the one rule for totals, and `AttemptUsage.Spend` totals the attempts started on its day.
`DirectorUnread` counts `budget-refused` and `advance-failed` by name; the `advance-failed` count in `TestDirectorUnreadCountsByType` was red against the list without it ([red run](../evidence/watcher-advance-and-wake-2026-10-22-red.txt)).

`SkipSyncForTests` makes every store a test binary opens afterwards skip bbolt's fsync of each commit ([windows-ci-speed](../decisions/windows-ci-speed.md)); records, receipts, locking and replay are unchanged and only a power loss could lose a commit.
It panics outside a test binary (`testing.Testing`), so production stores sync every commit.
The test binaries of `internal/store`, `messaging`, `interp`, `cli`, `launch`, `artisan` and `reconcile` call it from `TestMain`, which their own child processes run too; `cmd/guildhall`'s built binary syncs as production does.

The [attempt ownership contract](../store.md#attempt-ownership) defines monotonic recovery evidence, `ProbeLauncher`'s containment refusal and the explicit containment-aware recovery path.

The merge queue ([interpreter](../interpreter.md#the-merge-queue), [decision](../decisions/merge-queue-rebase.md)) lives in buckets `merge-queue` and `merge-holds`, keyed by repository and created on first use, so the schema stays 1.
`JoinMergeQueue` queues an open task under the decision or authorization id that allowed its merge, taking as its ready time, the first time only, the commit time of the receipt with the record it names (`decision/<id>` or `document/<name>`); a rejoin keeps it.
`LeaveMergeQueue` takes a task out, keeping its entry and ready time with `keepReady`, as after a build send-back, or removing it, as after a merge or cancellation, and releases the front it holds.
`ClaimMergeFront` makes the first queued, unheld task in `OrderMergeQueue` order (priority, ready time, task id) the front in one mutation, refusing any other with `ErrMergeQueued` and recording nothing, and counts its stays; `ReleaseMergeFront` gives the front up and leaves the task queued.
`MergeQueue` reads a repository's queue in one transaction, front first; a front whose task left or is closed does not stand, and a held front stands until its task releases it at its next advance, so no other task claims the front while the held one may still be pushing or merging.
`SetMergeHold` records a task's hold-merge with its history, receipt record `merge-hold`; the queue's own receipts name `merge-queue`.
The message types `merge-queued`, `rebased`, `rebase-escalated`, `rebase-stuck` and `approval-carried` are registered in `MessageTypes` as the engine's.

The engine-origin `pr-blocked` message type is registered for Director inbox counting and wake delivery.
Its head/state replay identity and observation document are owned by the interpreter; no new store mutation or schema is introduced.
`SendMessage` keeps its semantics: the same request replays its receipt and a different request under the same id is `ErrMutationConflict`. The interpreter treats that conflict, or `ErrExists`, on a Director message as already reported, so a re-sent message whose text changed never fails an advance ([interpreter](../interpreter.md)).
